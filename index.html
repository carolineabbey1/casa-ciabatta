Welcome to Casa Ciabatta

Your neighborhood spot for fresh-baked bread, artisan sandwiches, and homemade Italian favorites.

What Makes Our Ciabatta Special

Every morning at 4am, our bakers begin a tradition that's been passed down through three generations of the Rossi family. We use only four ingredients—flour, water, salt, and our 35-year-old sourdough starter—to create bread with the perfect balance of crispy crust and soft, airy interior.

Our ciabatta is never rushed. Each loaf undergoes a slow, 24-hour fermentation process that develops complex flavors and that signature chewy texture you won't find anywhere else. We bake in small batches throughout the day, so whether you stop by at opening or in the afternoon, you're always getting bread at its freshest.

Featured Sandwiches

Prosciutto & Fresh Mozzarella

Thinly sliced prosciutto di Parma layered with house-made mozzarella, vine-ripened tomatoes, and fresh basil, finished with a drizzle of aged balsamic. Simple, classic, unforgettable.

Chicken Ciapini

Our take on the Italian classic—herb-marinated grilled chicken with roasted red peppers, provolone, and garlic aioli on a pressed ciabatta roll. A longtime customer favorite.

Roasted Chicken Pesto

Tender roasted chicken breast with our scratch-made basil pesto, sun-dried tomatoes, and baby arugula. Bright, herby, and deeply satisfying.
