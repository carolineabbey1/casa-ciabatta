Additional Info

Parking

Street parking is available on Bakery Lane and surrounding streets. There's a metered lot one block east on Mulberry Street with two-hour parking. On weekends, street parking is free after 6pm.

Nearby Landmarks

We're located two blocks south of the Spring Street subway station (6 train) and one block west of Little Italy's main strip. Look for the red awning next to Marino's Barbershop—we've been neighbors for over 30 years.
